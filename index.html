Deploymate Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Infinity Care Partners collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information through our Deploymate mobile application.

The App is designed exclusively for Professionals who are relocating to the United States facilitated by Infinity Care partners to their respective employers/petitioners. We do NOT intend to collect personal health information , protected health information -do not upload or submit any confidential clinical information on this app

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please contact your deployment specialist.

Portal Access: Access to the App is granted for a limited period — specifically, up to 48 hours after your confirmed arrival in the United States. After this window, your session will expire automatically.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information in connection with your use of the App:

a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Email address (used for OTP-based authentication)
  • One-Time Password (OTP) for login verification
  • Messages and queries submitted via the in-app Travel Assistant chat

b) Information Retrieved from Our System

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Primary and alternate email addresses
  • Phone number
  • Flight itinerary and travel details (departure/arrival dates, airlines, flight numbers, layovers, port of entry)
  • Arrival appointment information
  • Assigned concierge name, phone, and photo
  • Immigration and visa-related information (for display purposes)

c) Documents

  • Flight confirmation documents
  • Welcome appointment schedules
  • Other relocation-related documents stored on our secure servers

d) Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • Device type and operating system (iOS or Android)
  • Session authentication tokens (stored locally on your device)
  • Session activity timestamps
  • Arrival confirmation action and timestamp

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To verify your identity via email-based OTP and maintain secure session access.
  • Personalised Experience: To display your travel details, flight information, concierge contact, and arrival appointments within the App.
  • Document Access: To retrieve and display documents relevant to your relocation (flight confirmations, welcome appointments).
  • Travel Assistance: To power the in-app AI chat assistant, which uses your name, email, and relocation details to answer your questions accurately.
  • Arrival Notifications: To notify the Infinity Care Partners team when you confirm your arrival in the United States.
  • Support & Communication: To facilitate communication between you, your concierge, and the ICP operations team.
  • Security: To detect unauthorised access, expire sessions appropriately, and maintain the integrity of the portal.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within Infinity Care Partners: Your information is accessible to authorised ICP staff, including your designated concierge, deployment specialist, and operational support team, solely for the purpose of facilitating your relocation.
  • Legal Compliance: We may share information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request.
  • Safety: We may share information if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Infinity Care Partners, our candidates, or others.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your personal information is stored on secure servers. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage and are used solely to maintain your session.

We employ the following security measures:

  • JWT (JSON Web Token) based authentication with time-limited session tokens
  • HTTPS (TLS) encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our servers
  • Automatic session expiry 48 hours after confirmed arrival
  • Role-based access controls on all server-side APIs
  • OTP verification required at every login — no persistent passwords

While we implement industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your information to the best of our ability.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained in our system for the duration of your engagement with Infinity Care Partners, including your onboarding, placement, and employment period. Session tokens stored on your device are automatically invalidated 48 hours after arrival confirmation or upon logout, whichever occurs first.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right of Correction: Request that inaccurate or incomplete information be corrected.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for processing where consent is the legal basis.

8. Children's Privacy

The Candidate Travel Portal is intended exclusively for use by adult healthcare professionals (18 years of age and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

9. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with or references the following third-party services:

  • Google Docs Viewer: Used to render document files (PDFs, Word documents) within the App. Documents are passed as encoded URLs; no document content is stored by Google.
  • Expo (React Native framework): The App is built using the Expo platform. Expo may collect anonymised crash and diagnostic data per their own privacy policy.
  • Airline & flight tracking websites: External links to airline trackers open in a browser and are governed by those third parties' privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through the App.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
